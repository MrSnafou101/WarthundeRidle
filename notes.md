listing oh caracteristics of each vehicle type:
Common to all vehicles:
- All vehicles have a unique identifier (ID).
- All vehicles have a name.
- All vehicle have a type (e.g., AA, Medium, Heavy, Fighter, Bomber, Destroyer,...).
- All vehicle have a Country/nation of origin.
- All vehicle have a Rank and Battle rating (BR).
- All vehicle have a position in the tech tree.
- All vehicle have the caliber of the main weapon.

Ground vehicles:
- have multipel main guns. (same caliber)
- Have multiple weapon systems
- Have tracks or wheels.
- Max speed

Air vehicles:
- Engine type, propeller or jet.
- Has the ability to got Mach 1 or not.
- Max Speed. /!\ Speed depends on altitude and temperature so may not saty or need to specify the conditions.
- Max altitude.
- Can carry ground attack ordnance (bombs, rockets, missiles).

Naval vehicles:
- Displacement (tonnage).
- Numeber of main guns / turrets.
- Auxiliary armament (AA guns, torpedoes, etc.).
